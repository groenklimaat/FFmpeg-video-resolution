## 🌱 Why this tool?
Data storage and transmission require massive amounts of energy. By compressing videos by 10-15x with **zero noticeable quality loss** (using the H.264 codec and smart CRF settings), we can drastically reduce server loads, storage requirements, and ultimately, our digital carbon footprint. 

Ideal for archiving, surveillance footage, and everyday use.
